*Video, about 5 minutes · September 2026*

In this walkthrough I compare two versions of a healthcare dashboard built from the same Excel data. The first came from an AI add-in working on its own. The second is the one in my portfolio, where I used AI for parts of the back end, such as the calculated measures behind the charts, and did the rest myself. The AI could not build a proper data model, so I set up the linked fact and dimension tables by hand and drew on YouTube tutorials and my own research for the design. My takeaway is that AI works best as a starting point, and the finished product still depends on knowing how the pieces fit together.
